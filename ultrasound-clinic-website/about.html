About the Doctor

Tanova Sh.A.

Medical Doctor and Ultrasound Diagnostics Specialist

Dr. Tanova Sh.A. has extensive professional experience in the field of medicine since 1990 and has been working in the field of ultrasound diagnostics since 2007.

With many years of experience in both general medicine and ultrasound diagnostics, Dr. Tanova provides ultrasound examinations covering different areas of the body, including the abdominal organs, kidneys, thyroid gland, breasts, pelvic organs, and prostate gland.

Her approach to each examination is characterized by careful attention to detail and a thorough assessment of the findings. Through her professional experience, she contributes to the timely detection of possible abnormalities and diseases, providing patients with clear information that may support further medical evaluation and appropriate treatment.
