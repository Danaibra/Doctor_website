Ultrasound Diagnostics

Professional ultrasound diagnostic services provided by Dr. Tanova Sh.A., with extensive experience in medicine and ultrasound diagnostics.

Dr. Tanova has been working in the field of medicine since 1990 and has specialized in ultrasound diagnostics since 2007.

The clinic provides ultrasound examinations of different organs and systems using a professional and patient-oriented approach.

Our Services

  • Ultrasound of the abdominal organs (liver, pancreas, spleen, and gallbladder)
  • Ultrasound of the kidneys
  • Ultrasound of the thyroid gland
  • Ultrasound of the mammary glands
  • Ultrasound of the pelvic organs
  • Ultrasound of the prostate gland
