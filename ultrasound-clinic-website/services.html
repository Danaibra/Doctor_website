Services

Ultrasound of the Abdominal Organs

Used to assess the structure and condition of the liver, gallbladder, pancreas, spleen, and other abdominal organs.

Ultrasound of the Kidneys

A non-invasive examination of the size, structure, and general condition of the kidneys. It can show changes that may need further medical evaluation.

Ultrasound of the Thyroid Gland

A non-invasive examination of the structure and size of the thyroid gland. It can show changes or nodules that may need further evaluation by a healthcare professional.

Ultrasound of the Mammary Glands

A non-invasive examination of the structure of breast tissue. It can be part of a medical assessment when recommended by a healthcare professional.

Ultrasound of the Pelvic Organs

Used to examine structures in the pelvic area, such as the uterus, ovaries, and other pelvic organs, depending on the patient and the examination needed.

Ultrasound of the Prostate Gland

A non-invasive examination of the structure and size of the prostate gland. It may be recommended as part of a medical evaluation when clinically appropriate.
